Debezium Community Showcase

Giovanni Panice
June 24, 2026 · 2 min read
community meetings contributors events forum showcase

It has been a month since we opened our weekly team meeting to the community, and we have received many interesting insights. Because of that, we have decided to create another opportunity for the community to connect and share:

Welcome to the Debezium Community Showcase!

Starting next month, we will host a monthly showcase session that will be live-streamed on YouTube. Whether you would like to share something about CDC with Debezium, how you use Debezium in your work, or anything related to contributing to the project, you are welcome to submit a talk.

However, if there is not enough content for a given month, the showcase may be postponed to the following month.

Session Proposal and Participation

To keep track of talks, session proposals will be managed through Sessionize.

Community members are encouraged to first propose a session in our weekly forum for a brief evaluation, and then formally submit the proposal through Sessionize so that we can schedule it on the calendar.

Format of Sessions

We have two kind of sessions based on duration:

  • Lightning Talks: 10-15min duration

  • Standard talks: 30 duration

Calendar and Scheduling

The showcase will be scheduled on a Tuesday and will take place during the weekly forum, depending on the Debezium team’s schedule and the available content. The showcase will last up to 1 hour and may include one lightning talk and one standard talk, with a few additional minutes for questions.

We truly believe these sessions provide another great opportunity to share, discuss, and exchange ideas that help shape the future of Debezium.

We look forward to seeing your insights!

About Debezium

Debezium is an open source distributed platform that turns your existing databases into event streams, so applications can see and respond almost instantly to each committed row-level change in the databases. Debezium is built on top of Kafka and provides Kafka Connect compatible connectors that monitor specific database management systems. Debezium records the history of data changes in Kafka logs, so your application can be stopped and restarted at any time and can easily consume all of the events it missed while it was not running, ensuring that all events are processed correctly and completely. Debezium is open source under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

Get involved

We hope you find Debezium interesting and useful, and want to give it a try. Follow us on Twitter @debezium, chat with us on Zulip, or join our mailing list to talk with the community. All of the code is open source on GitHub, so build the code locally and help us improve ours existing connectors and add even more connectors. If you find problems or have ideas how we can improve Debezium, please let us know or log an issue.
