{%- endif -%} {%- comment -%} Tags, most used first. site.tags is a map of name to post list, and Liquid cannot sort a map by a computed value such as the length of that list. The usual workaround: build "0233|mysql" strings with the count zero padded to a fixed width, sort them lexicographically, reverse, then split each back apart. Four digits is ample - the busiest tag here has 233 posts. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign tagPairs = "" | split: "," -%} {%- for tag in site.tags -%} {%- assign padded = tag[1].size | prepend: "0000" -%} {%- assign padded = padded | slice: -4, 4 -%} {%- assign pair = padded | append: "|" | append: tag[0] -%} {%- assign tagPairs = tagPairs | push: pair -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign sortedTags = tagPairs | sort | reverse -%}
