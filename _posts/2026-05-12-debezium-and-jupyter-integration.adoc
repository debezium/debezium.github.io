The Debezium community grows through open collaboration among users, contributors, maintainers, and the wider data-streaming ecosystem.

Starting *Tuesday, May 19*, we are opening our weekly team meeting on *every Tuesday* to the broader Debezium community. 
Whether you are a contributor, evaluating Debezium, or simply interested in change data capture and streaming, you are welcome to attend.

The meeting duration is scheduled for 45 minutes. However, to respect everyone’s time, meetings may conclude earlier if the agenda is completed and there are no external attendees waiting for discussion. 
The minimum meeting duration will be 15 minutes.

== Meeting Agenda and Participation

To keep the meetings transparent and collaborative, the agenda is maintained in a shared Google Document that is open for external comments and suggestions.

You can view the agenda document here:

https://docs.google.com/document/d/1f3RlmbI9N7lto8C3HQW_8RBo0dz6jTKZUzmAwPWvGFc/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.rkrkme2bx1oe[Debezium Community Meeting Agenda]

Community members are encouraged to propose topics, discussion items, demos, questions, or updates ahead of the meeting.

New discussion topics should first be proposed on Zulip so the community 
and maintainers can help shape the discussion and determine whether it should be added to an upcoming meeting agenda.

== Calendar and Scheduling

Debezium community meetings and events are listed in our shared Google Calendar. You can browse the schedule online or subscribe to the public iCalendar feed to keep upcoming events in your own calendar application.

Public calendar:

https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/embed?src=debezium%40gmail.com&ctz=Europe%2FPrague[Debezium Community Calendar]

We hope these meetings provide another accessible way for the community to collaborate, exchange ideas, discuss roadmap items, highlight ongoing work, and help shape the future of Debezium together.

We look forward to seeing you there!
