We’re excited to share the next milestone in Debezium’s Google Summer of Code 2026 journey: our contributors have been selected, and the community bonding period has officially started! 🎉

Earlier this year, https://debezium.io/blog/2026/02/23/gsoc-announce/[we announced] that Debezium would participate in Google Summer of Code 2026 as part of the JBoss Community organization, offering project ideas focused on change data capture, vector databases, Quarkus, AI/ML, developer tooling, and the Debezium platform.
Since then, we’ve had many interesting conversations with prospective contributors, reviewed proposals, and worked together to select the projects that will move forward this year.

The official GSoC 2026 timeline announced accepted contributors on April 30th, followed by the community bonding period from May 1st to May 24th. Coding will officially begin on May 25th.

== Meet Our GSoC 2026 Contributors

We’re happy to welcome the contributors to the Debezium community for the following projects:

**summary**: Debezium CLI would provide developers and operators with a streamlined way to create, manage, and monitor CDC pipelines across data systems.

**summary**: The project idea is to provide support for running Debezium on bare metal, virtual machines, and cloud services, using Debezium Server distributions (container images and standalone packages).

== A Big Thank You to All Applicants

We received many thoughtful proposals and had great discussions with prospective contributors.
We want to thank everyone who took the time to learn about Debezium, join the community channels, ask questions, prepare an application and a special thanks to all who submitted any patch as part of getting familiar with Debezium.

If your proposal was not selected this year, we still encourage you to stay involved.
GSoC is only one way to contribute to Debezium.
The project is open all year round, and we’re always happy to welcome new contributors through issues, documentation improvements, bug fixes, discussions, and feature proposals.

== What's next

We’re looking forward to a productive and exciting summer of open-source collaboration.
The selected projects will help expand the Debezium ecosystem and improve the experience for developers working with change data capture.
As coding begins, we’ll share updates about the progress of each project and highlight the work our contributors are doing.

Welcome to GSoC 2026 contributors for Debezium, we’re excited to have you with us! 🚀
