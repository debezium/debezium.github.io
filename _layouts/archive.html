--- layout: base --- {%- comment -%} Blog archive: every post, newest first, grouped by year and month. The previous version tracked a running counter and then re-walked site.posts with `limit: counter offset: forloop.index | minus: counter` to pull each month back out. group_by_exp does the grouping directly, which removes the arithmetic and the double traversal, and gives a real count per group. Grouping preserves the order of the input, and site.posts is newest first, so years and months both come out descending without an explicit sort. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign byYear = site.posts | group_by_exp: "post", "post.date | date: '%Y'" -%}
